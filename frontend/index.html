Multi-Layer Security Architecture

Defense-in-depth inspection pipeline protecting against sophisticated email threat vectors.

Dual ML Threat Classifiers

Neural TF-IDF and Naive Bayes models trained on 5,500+ samples evaluate spam & phishing probabilities with high accuracy.

Anti-BEC Impersonation Shield

Detects display name spoofing and executive role impersonation from external freemail providers with critical red alerts.

Time-of-Click SafeLinks

Rewrites links to dynamically verify destination reputation upon click, blocking IP connections and suspicious TLDs.

Data Loss Prevention (DLP)

Automatically inspects outbound and inbound bodies for PAN, Aadhaar, SSN, and Credit Cards with character masking.

Domain Authentication Engine

Rigorous validation of SPF, DKIM, and DMARC DNS policies to intercept unauthorized relays and spoofed domains.

Sandboxed HTML Previews

Inspect flagged emails in isolated sandboxed iframes with scripts defanged, preventing drive-by payload triggers.
